# Hi, I'm Ellie Bastos Degobi

**Psychometrics • Metascience • Psychological Assessment • Data Science**

I'm a quantitative psychologist and data scientist working at the intersection of **psychometrics, psychological assessment, metascience, and open science**.

My research focuses on improving how psychological and behavioral sciences **measure constructs, analyze data, evaluate evidence, and make research decisions**. I am particularly interested in developing methods and tools that make research more reliable, transparent, interpretable, and reproducible.

## 🔬 Research Interests

* **Psychometrics & Measurement**

  * Latent variable models
  * Psychological measurement
  * Reliability and validity
  * Item Response Theory
  * Measurement invariance
  * Measurement level and model assumptions

* **Metascience & Open Science**

  * Questionable research practices
  * Reproducibility and replicability
  * Open science interventions
  * Researcher decision-making
  * Gamification and behavioral interventions for open science
  * Improving research practices beyond policy-level changes

* **Psychological Assessment**

  * Development and validation of psychological instruments
  * Mental health assessment
  * Eating-disorder assessment
  * Cross-cultural adaptation of measures
  * Evidence-based assessment

* **Quantitative Methods & Data Science**

  * R and Python
  * Structural Equation Modeling
  * Causal inference
  * Simulation studies
  * Network analysis
  * Reproducible research workflows
  * Data visualization and research software

## 🧭 What I Work On

My quantitative background allows me to approach data science from a **measurement-first perspective**.

Rather than focusing only on predictive accuracy, I am interested in whether the variables being analyzed are measured appropriately, whether statistical assumptions are defensible, and whether results can be interpreted reliably.

Current projects include work on:

* psychometric theory and measurement;
* reliability and validity assessment;
* open science and metascience interventions;
* researcher decision-making and questionable research practices;
* rationality and media literacy;
* psychological and mental health assessment;
* reproducible statistical tools and educational resources.
